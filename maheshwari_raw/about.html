About Us

About Us

Maheshwari & Co. stands at the forefront as a full-service Indian corporate law firm, recognized for its services as corporate legal consultants in India and for foreign joint ventures. With a PAN India presence and headquarters in New Delhi, our reach extends through branch offices in Mumbai and Lucknow alongside network offices in almost all major Indian cities. 

Our team, a dynamic mix of experienced attorneys and bright, innovative minds, bringing a fresh perspective to each case. We are committed to excellence, but our drive doesn’t stop there. It’s about building lasting relationships, understanding your unique challenges and working tirelessly to turn those challenges into opportunities.

Why Choose Us

Achieving Legal Excellence Together

At Maheshwari & Co., we are a cohesive team of dedicated legal professionals, united by a shared vision of providing unparalleled legal services across a variety of sectors. Our expertise spans Corporate Law, Foreign Investment, Employment and Labour Laws and many more.   

Driven by a passion for legal excellence and a commitment to our clients, we combine time-bound practices with innovative strategies to navigate the complexities of law in a global context. Each member of our team brings unique insights and solutions, making Maheshwari & Co. a place where tradition meets innovation. Here, every challenge is met with a tailored solution, ensuring that our clients not only succeed but thrive.

Our Mission

Our mission is to deliver comprehensive legal solutions to our clients, ensuring clarity, security and success in their business endeavours. We stand on our foundation of ethical practice and open communication. It’s not just about solving problems; it’s about empowering your success every step of the way.

Our Vision

Imagine a world where legal services are seamlessly integrated into your business, where excellence and ethics aren’t just ideals, but the standard. That’s the world we’re creating at Maheshwari & Co. We envision ourselves as more than just a legal firm; we aim to be your trusted partners in growth, both in India and globally. We aim to foster long-term relationships with our clients, guiding them through the complexities of the legal landscape towards achieving their business objectives.

  • Partners in growth
  • Global Presence
  • Sustained Investment
  • Building a trusted relationship
  • Deeper understanding and social responsibility
